\chapter{The driver: speeds to the robot}
\label{ch:t-driver}

\section{From a velocity to an axis command}

\code{x30_driver} turns the mux's \topic{cmd_vel} (base\_link, REP-103: $x$ forward, $y$ left,
counter-clockwise positive) into the robot's axis commands, streamed at \SI{50}{Hz} to the motion
host, only while the robot is in its stepping state and its motion report is fresh. Each axis is
scaled to the maximum speed of the gait the robot walks in:

\begin{equation}
  u = \operatorname{round}\!\left(\frac{v}{v_{\max}(\text{gait})}\cdot 32767\right),
  \qquad |u| < 655 \;\Rightarrow\; u = \pm 656
\end{equation}

The lateral and yaw axes go out negated (the robot counts right as positive). A value inside the
robot's dead zone ($|u| < 655$) would be ignored by the robot, so a small non-zero command goes
out as the smallest value the robot moves on: the X30 creeps rather than stands.

\noindent Speeds at normal body height (application manual, Appendix B); at crawl height Walk and
Slope go forward at half. Run and Silent have no published speeds: the driver does not move the
robot in them unless \param{gait_limits} gives them, for example
\cmd{gait_limits: {silent: [0.5, 0.5, 0.2, 0.5]}}.

\begin{warnbox}[The gait limits the stack]
The robot profile's limits must hold in every gait the robot walks in during a patrol. In
Off-road the X30 walks at most \SI{0.3}{m/s} forward whatever the follower asks: the patrol is
slower, still safe (the braking model assumed more), but the follower's progress checks wait
longer. Keep Walk for patrols, or lower \param{cruise}.
\end{warnbox}

\section{The signs of the axes}

A wrong sign makes the robot step right when the stack means left: every avoidance and the
protective field's sideways check go wrong with it. Check once per robot, in REMOTE, with the
robot standing in a free area and the handheld app closed (one source drives at a time):

\begin{lstlisting}[language=bash]
rosservice call /control/set_mode "{mode: remote, source: handheld}"
rostopic pub -r 10 /control/remote/handheld/cmd_vel geometry_msgs/Twist '{linear: {y: 0.1}}'
\end{lstlisting}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item With the second line running, the robot must step to its \textbf{left}. If it steps right:
    \cmd{invert_vy: true} in \file{driver_params.yaml}.
  \item The same with \cmd{'{angular: {z: 0.2}}'}: the robot must turn \textbf{counter-clockwise}
    (left). If not: \cmd{invert_wz: true}.
  \item Ctrl-C, then back to AUTO: \cmd{rosservice call /control/set_mode "{mode: auto}"}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Silence and stopping}

\begin{description}
  \item[\param{cmd_timeout_sec} (\SI{0.5}{s})] a command older than this is replaced by zeros;
  \item[\param{stop_burst_sec} (\SI{0.5}{s})] zeros streamed after motion ends, then silence (the
    robot itself drops an axis that falls silent for \SI{1}{s});
  \item[Episodes] the first command after a quiet period starts an episode: the driver puts the
    robot into the control mode and the stepping state it needs (\param{auto_control_mode},
    \param{auto_stepping}), three tries in two seconds. Nothing moves the robot after
    \cmd{sit_down}, an emergency stop, a fall or a takeover by Deep Robotics' handheld until the
    command has been zero or quiet once.
\end{description}

The driver's own clamps (\param{max_vx} \SI{1.0}{m/s}, \param{max_vy} \SI{0.5}{m/s},
\param{max_wz} \SI{1.0}{rad/s}) are a last bound above the profile; the profile's limits are the
ones the stack plans with.
