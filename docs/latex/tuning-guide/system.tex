\chapter{Health, the handheld and NETRA}
\label{ch:t-system}

\section{The health monitor}

\code{health_monitor_node} publishes \topic{health/status} every second (and
\topic{/diagnostics}): every finding with its level, and a speed scale.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright}p{0.3\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Check} & \textbf{Issue when} \\
  \midrule
  \param{nodes.expected} & a node of the host is missing (filled by bringup from what it starts) \\
  \param{sensors.topics} & a topic below \param{rate_tolerance} of its rate (\topic{/livox/lidar}
    \SI{5}{Hz}, \topic{/Odometry} \SI{8}{Hz}) or its stamps too old \\
  \param{state.*} & odometry LOST (error) or DEGRADED, localization NONE, the e-stop engaged or
    the protective field stale or blind (errors) \\
  \param{tf.*} & a dynamic transform older than \param{max_age_sec}, or from the future (clocks) \\
  \param{system.*} & the hottest thermal zone above \SI{85}{\celsius}, memory above \SI{92}{\percent},
    less than \SI{5}{GB} free, a load above 2 per CPU \\
  \param{clock.*} & (off) \cmd{chronyc tracking} offset above \SI{50}{ms} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

For \param{startup_grace_sec} (\SI{30}{s}) after the start, missing nodes and inputs only warn.

\subsection{The speed scale}

An error makes the scale 0; a warning takes the lowest scale of the
\param{degradation.rules} it matches (\code{sensor:/livox/lidar=0.5}, \code{sensor:/Odometry=0.5},
\code{tf:*=0.5}), otherwise \param{warn_scale} (1). The control mux applies it to every command only
with \cmd{use_health_limit: true} in \file{x30_control/config/control.yaml}; the X30 ships with it off,
and the follower pauses by itself on the conditions that matter (stale poses and scans, NONE). Turn
it on to make a slow LiDAR or odometry halve the speed instead of merely reporting it.

\section{The handheld gateway}

\code{teleop_udp_node} serves the H12 on UDP \param{port} 9870, with the shared secret of
\code{X30_TELEOP_TOKEN}.

\begin{description}
  \item[\param{max_vx}, \param{max_vy}, \param{max_wz}] the stick's full deflection, never above the
    profile's limits (each axis is capped separately): a full stick sideways asks for \param{max_vy}.
  \item[\param{client_timeout_sec}] a handheld silent this long is dropped and its stick zeroed; the
    mux's \param{remote_timeout_sec} stops the robot earlier.
  \item[\param{camera_fps}, \param{camera_jpeg_quality}, \param{camera_max_width}] the video to the
    handheld: lower them on a weak link before anything else.
  \item[\param{lidar_hz}, \param{lidar_voxel}, \param{lidar_max_points}] the cloud on the handheld's
    map.
  \item[\param{patrol_speed_min}, \param{patrol_speed_max}] the range of the patrol speed the app
    sets (the follower's \param{cruise}).
\end{description}

\section{The NETRA uplink}

\code{fleet_uplink_node} (navigation host) connects the robot to NETRA at \param{server_url} with
its \param{robot_id} and the token of \code{X30_NETRA_TOKEN}.

\begin{description}
  \item[\param{telemetry_hz}] snapshots to NETRA (\SI{1}{Hz}).
  \item[\param{spool_dir}, \param{upload_period_sec}] routes and inspections wait on disk until
    NETRA confirms them, tried every \SI{5}{s}: a patrol without network loses nothing.
  \item[\param{clock_sync_period_sec}, \param{clock_warn_ms}] the offset to NETRA's clock, measured
    every \SI{10}{s} and reported with the telemetry; above \SI{200}{ms} the robot warns: set the
    robot's clock right (chrony).
\end{description}

\section{The simulator}

\code{x30_sim} replaces the driver, the LiDARs, the IMU and FAST-LIO with a planar simulation that
publishes the same topics: routes, the follower, avoidance, the field and the inspection run
unchanged. Its parameters (Appendix~\ref{ch:t-reference}) set the world, the noise and the
robot's response; tune the stack's logic there, never its safety distances, which belong to the
real robot's measurements.
