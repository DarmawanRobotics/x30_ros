\section{Conditions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The substation's permit for the test is in place; the routes avoid every bay under work.
  \item A person trained on the X30 User Manual operates the robot; one person stands by the hard
    emergency stop or Deep Robotics' handheld during every test with motion.
  \item The battery is charged; the robot starts on its dock; NETRA runs on its final computer.
  \item Equipment: a tape measure, a stopwatch, a box \qtyproduct{0.5 x 0.5 x 0.5}{m}, a box
    \SI{10}{cm} high, a contact thermometer or a calibrated handheld thermal camera, a heat source
    (a hot-water bottle), a phone for the alerts.
\end{itemize}

\section{How to record}

Tick \checkbox~Pass or \checkbox~Fail for each test and write what was measured on its line. A
failed test goes to the punch list (Chapter~\ref{ch:s-signoff}) with its ID; the decision at the end
says whether it blocks the acceptance.
