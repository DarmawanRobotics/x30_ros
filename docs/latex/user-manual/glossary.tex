\chapter{Words used here}
\label{ch:u-glossary}

\begin{description}
  \item[AUTO, REMOTE] who drives: the route (AUTO) or the operator's sticks (REMOTE).
  \item[Dock, start spot] where the robot starts; the origin of its routes.
  \item[E-stop] the software emergency stop; the robot is held still until released.
  \item[Inspection point] a place, at a stop, where the camera looks at equipment.
  \item[NETRA] the command center.
  \item[Partial discharge (PD)] small electrical discharges in insulation, heard by the acoustic
    camera.
  \item[Protective field] the space around the robot it keeps free: it slows and stops for
    obstacles.
  \item[Route] a walk taught once and repeated.
  \item[Site] a substation's routes, tags and inspection points.
  \item[Stop point] a place on a route where the robot stands and inspects.
  \item[Tag] a printed marker that corrects the robot's position.
\end{description}
