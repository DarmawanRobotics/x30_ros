\section{Every command through one gate}

Only \code{control_mux_node} publishes \topic{/x30/cmd_vel}. Navigation (\topic{control/nav_cmd_vel})
and the operator sources (\topic{control/remote/<source>/cmd_vel}) publish to it; it forwards one of
them, AUTO or REMOTE, after the envelope, the health speed scale (optional) and the protective
field, and holds zeros during an e-stop. The follower has its own guard on the same scan, with
margins at least the field's: in normal operation the field never has to act.

\section{What is not certified}

The e-stops of the H12 and NETRA, the protective field and the guard are software on a computer
without a safety rating. They reduce risk; the hard emergency stop on the robot, Deep Robotics'
handheld and a responsible operator are the protection, and the site's risk assessment decides
where the robot may walk.
