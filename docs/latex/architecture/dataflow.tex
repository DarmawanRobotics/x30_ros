\section{Transforms}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{tf}
\end{center}

\begin{description}
  \item[$\T{map}{odom}$] \code{localization_node}: identity at the start (the start spot is the
    origin), corrected by tags. A correction is published as the old value just before its time
    and the new one at it, so a lookup between two corrections never interpolates across a jump.
  \item[$\T{odom}{base\_link}$] the localization's bridge of FAST-LIO: FAST-LIO's pose levelled by
    gravity at the start (the IMU's up direction gives roll and pitch), so \code{odom}'s $z$ is
    up even when the robot started on a slope.
  \item[base\_link to the sensors] \code{robot_state_publisher} from the URDF; the LiDAR's frame
    from \code{lidar_pose}.
\end{description}
