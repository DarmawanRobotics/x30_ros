\section{On disk}

\begin{lstlisting}
~/x30/map/<site>/            a site: a substation (1-48 of a-z, 0-9, _ and -)
  tags.json                  recorded tags: their map poses
  routes/<name>.csv          taught routes: x, y (m, map frame), yaw_deg, dwell_sec
  inspection.yaml            the site's own inspection points and targets
  netra/<route_id>.csv       routes NETRA started a patrol on
  netra/inspection.yaml      NETRA's plan for them (points netra_stop<k>, targets netra_<id>)
~/x30/reports/<day>/...      inspection reports
~/x30/recordings/<day>/<time>_<reason>/   recorder sessions (perception host)
~/x30/spool/                 uploads waiting for NETRA
~/x30/logs/                  each node's JSON lines log
\end{lstlisting}

A row with \code{dwell_sec} above zero is a stop point. Files are written atomically (a temporary
file renamed), so a power cut never leaves half a route.

\begin{center}
\begin{small}
\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{0.2\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.72\linewidth}@{}}
  \toprule
  State & Meaning \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \bottomrule
  \endfoot
  INACTIVE & no route running \\
  JOINING & walking onto the route from up to \param{join.max_distance} away \\
  FOLLOWING & on the route, at the speed profile's speed \\
  APPROACHING & converging onto a stop point or the route's end \\
  DWELLING & standing at a stop point: the inspection runs \\
  PAUSED & a pause reason holds it: the operator, the e-stop, REMOTE, localization (NONE), the
    battery, disarmed \\
  BLOCKED & standing still too long: an obstacle path shift, detours and backing up could not pass \\
  OFF\_PATH & farther from the route than \param{off_path_stop} \\
  GOAL\_REACHED & the route's end \\
  FAILED & a problem the follower cannot leave by itself \\
\end{longtable}
\end{small}
\end{center}

\noindent The follower is a pure state machine: \code{Follower.tick(inputs) -> Decision} every
\SI{50}{ms}, with the scan, the pose, the avoidance output and the time as its inputs; the node
only gathers them and publishes the decision. Every state may go to INACTIVE (stop) or FAILED.
